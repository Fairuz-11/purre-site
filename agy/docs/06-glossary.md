# 06 — Glossary & Domain Terms

## Domain Concepts

- **PUREE**: The brand and business identity for "PUREE Construction & Design" (Malang, Indonesia).
- **Kontraktor Bangunan**: Building contractor services responsible for executing physical construction and structural works.
- **Renovasi Bangunan**: Renovation services for structural upgrading, spatial overhaul, and refurbishment of existing buildings.
- **Desain Arsitektur**: Architectural design services covering conceptual design, spatial planning, facade design, and construction drawing documentation.
- **Desain Interior**: Interior architecture and space planning including material palettes, built-in furniture, lighting, and ambient design.

## Technical Terms

- **SSG (Static Site Generation)**: Pre-rendering static HTML pages at build time.
- **`generateStaticParams`**: Next.js App Router method that pre-populates dynamic segment routes (`[slug]`) at build time.
- **Turbopack**: High-performance Rust-based bundler for Next.js, with Tailwind CSS v4 support via `@tailwindcss/turbopack`.
- **Motion (`motion/react`)**: Modern Framer Motion package import syntax for animation primitives.
