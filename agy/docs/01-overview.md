# 01 — Project Overview

## What is PUREE?

**PUREE** (`purre-site`) is the official web presence for an Indonesian construction and architectural design firm based in Malang, East Java. The business provides integrated services across commercial and residential building contracting, structural renovation, architectural conceptualization, and interior design.

## Core Purpose & Audience

- **Primary Goal**: Showcase the company's capabilities, design philosophy, past portfolio projects, and service workflow to attract prospective residential and commercial clients.
- **Audience**: Homeowners, property developers, commercial businesses, and corporate decision-makers seeking professional construction, architectural, and renovation services.
- **Tone & Aesthetic**: "Clean Industrial / Modern Corporate" characterized by structured typography (Barlow + Inter), high-contrast neutrals (#F7F7F5 background, #1F1F1F dark accents), and brand red highlights (#C90000).

## High-Level Capabilities

1. **Brand Showcase & Narrative**: Detailed overview of company values, philosophy, and history (`/tentang`).
2. **Service Catalog & Detailed Process**: 4 core service lines with dedicated detail landing pages explaining work stages and features (`/layanan` and `/layanan/[slug]`).
3. **Filterable Portfolio**: Curated gallery of completed and ongoing projects with category filtering and dynamic project detail views (`/portofolio` and `/portofolio/[slug]`).
4. **Client Consultation Funnel**: Interactive intake form for project inquiries with type, location, and requirement validation (`/kontak`).
